--- layout: AURA-MF title: SimV2 — Sandia Empirical PV Model ---

Executive Summary

SimV2 implements the Sandia Empirical PV Model (King, Boyson & Kratochvil 2004) for fast, calibration-driven panel temperature and power prediction. It serves as the primary medium-accuracy tier in the fidelity ladder — faster than SimV1 while maintaining broad validity across operating conditions. SimV2b extends the model with an IIR filter to smooth rapidly-changing irradiance inputs.

Sandia Module Temperature Model

Module and Cell Temperature

The Sandia model predicts module temperature from ambient conditions and plane-of-array irradiance:

$$T_{\text{module}} = T_{\text{amb}} + G_{\text{poa}} \cdot e^{a + b \cdot u_\infty}$$

$$T_{\text{cell}} = T_{\text{module}} + \frac{G_{\text{poa}}}{G_{\text{STC}}} \cdot \Delta T$$

where:

  • $a$, $b$ — empirical mount-type coefficients (e.g., open-rack glass/glass: $a = -3.56$, $b = -0.075$)
  • $\Delta T \approx 3$ °C — cell-to-module temperature difference at STC
  • $G_{\text{STC}} = 1000$ W m$^{-2}$ — standard test condition irradiance
  • $u_\infty$ — wind speed [m s$^{-1}$]

Sandia Power Correction

Maximum Power at Operating Conditions

SimV2 corrects the STC maximum power for operating irradiance and temperature:

$$P_{\text{mp}} = \frac{G_{\text{poa}}}{G_{\text{ref}}} P_{\text{mp,ref}} \left[1 + \gamma_{\text{Pmp}} (T_{\text{cell}} - T_{\text{ref}})\right]$$

where:

  • $\gamma_{\text{Pmp}} \approx -0.0045$ °C$^{-1}$ — temperature coefficient for crystalline silicon
  • $T_{\text{ref}} = 25$ °C — STC reference temperature
  • $G_{\text{ref}} = 1000$ W m$^{-2}$ — STC reference irradiance

Spectral Correction

Air Mass Modifier

SimV2 applies a spectral correction factor $f_1(AM)$ as a cubic polynomial in air mass (AM) to account for the spectral distribution of incident irradiance deviating from AM1.5:

$$f_1(AM) = a_0 + a_1 \cdot AM + a_2 \cdot AM^2 + a_3 \cdot AM^3$$

Coefficients $a_0$–$a_3$ are module-specific and determined from outdoor characterisation data.

SimV2b: IIR Filter Variant

Exponential Moving Average Filter

SimV2b adds an IIR (exponential moving average) filter to smooth rapidly-changing irradiance inputs before passing them to the Sandia model:

$$G_{\text{filtered}}(t) = \lambda\, G_{\text{poa}}(t) + (1-\lambda)\, G_{\text{filtered}}(t-1), \quad \lambda \in (0,1)$$

Smaller $\lambda$ values produce heavier smoothing and are suited to sensors with noisy irradiance readings or rapidly varying cloud cover. This makes SimV2b the recommended tier for high-frequency data streams.

Role in Fidelity Ladder

SimV2/V2b occupies the middle tier of the 8-tier AURA-MPC stack:

SimV2 is the first tier that provides calibration-driven accuracy without the computational cost of solving a full transient ODE (that is handled by SimV3/V3b). It is faster than SimV1 by several orders of magnitude while remaining valid across a broad range of irradiance and temperature conditions.
