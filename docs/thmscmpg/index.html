Ongoing Research

Comparing Seven Methods for Determining the Refractive Index of a Glass Prism

Working with Dr. Eugenii Donev, we compare seven methods for determining the refractive index of a glass prism across five laser wavelengths, judging each on accuracy and pedagogical value for undergraduate physics. Methods include minimum deviation, Fresnel reflectance, fitting to the minimum deviation curve, Brewster’s angle, Rotating Analyzer Ellipsometry (RAE), and total internal reflection. We are preparing the paper for the European Journal of Physics.

AURA-MPC: Model Predictive Control for Photovoltaic Panel Orientation

An independent research project funded by the APSU Department of Physics, Engineering, and Astronomy. I built a model predictive controller for a 3-axis solar panel mount that uses a neural surrogate of a lumped thermal model, trained on data from an RK4 generator written in Fortran. On a year of Sandia National Laboratories module data, a physics-calibrated model beats persistence for 15-minute panel temperature forecasts (2.21 K vs 3.20 K RMSE), and in a closed-loop sandbox the controller captures 98.7% of oracle energy.

Current progress: Building the data acquisition system to collect thermocouple data on our own panel in Clarksville, TN.
